Alternate Key — Interactive Simulator

Understand what happens to candidate keys that are not selected as the primary key.

1. Alternate key in simple words

A table may have more than one candidate key. The designer chooses one candidate key as the primary key. The remaining candidate keys are called alternate keys.

Example: If both StudentID and Email uniquely identify every student, and StudentID is chosen as the primary key, then Email is an alternate key.
